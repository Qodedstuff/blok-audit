3
Add a Live Status Badge so Nobody is Confused
✅ Done Hero.tsx
🚦
Think of it like this: A traffic light with no colours. Is it red? Green? Nobody knows if they should stop or go. The site said "Live since spring 2024" in one place, then "First Public Launch Q1 2026" somewhere else, and "Mainnet Protocol Launch Q3 2026" in another place. A visitor had three different answers to the question "Is this live?" We added one clear green badge at the top that answers it instantly.
❌ Before
Three conflicting signals:
Hero "Live since spring 2024"
Q1 '26 "First Public Launch"
Q3 '26 "Mainnet Protocol Launch"
⚠️ So... is it live or not?
✅ After
One clear answer at the top:
Beta Live on Arbitrum
Now Beta is live ✓
Q3 '26 Full mainnet launch
✓ Visitor knows exactly where things stand

5
Fix the Token Launch Date Contradiction
✅ Done TokenTeaser.tsx
📅
Think of it like this: A cinema posts "Movie starts at 3pm" on the door but the ticket says "Movie starts at 9pm." Which one is right? Nobody knows. The BLOK Capital site said the token IDO launches "Q1 2026" in the token section but the roadmap says "Q2 2027." That's over a year of difference. An investor checking this would immediately lose confidence. One date needs to change to match the other.
❌ Before
$BLOKC Token
Governance · Rewards · Access
Launches at IDO · Q1 2026
⚠️ But roadmap says Q2 2027 — contradiction!
✅ After
$BLOKC Token
Governance · Rewards · Access
Launches at IDO · Q2 2027
✓ Now matches roadmap — no confusion
6
Add a Status Banner to the Roadmap Section
✅ Done RoadmapTrack.tsx
🗺️
Think of it like this: A map that shows you lots of roads and destinations — but doesn't show a "You Are Here" marker. You can see where things are going but you don't know where you currently stand. The roadmap had dates and milestones but no clear "here's where we are TODAY" marker. We added a status banner at the top of the roadmap so visitors immediately see the current state.
❌ Before
Protocol Roadmap
2024 Private Sale Done
Q1 '26 First Public Launch Live?
Q3 '26 Mainnet Launch Soon
⚠️ No "You Are Here" — visitor is lost
✅ After
Protocol Roadmap
Beta Live ⏳ Mainnet: Q3 2026 Current status
2024 Private Sale Done ✓
Q1 '26 First Public Launch Live ✓
Q3 '26 Mainnet Launch Coming
✓ Visitor knows exactly where they are
